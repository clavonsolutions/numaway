# Refund Policy — Numaway Education Services Limited

**Effective date:** 2026-04-27
**Cooling-off period:** 7 days
**Processing time:** 10 business days
**Status:** DRAFT — Counsel review required

## Free Services
Many Numaway services are free for students. No refund applies.

## Paid Services
Where fees are charged for premium services or non-partner university applications:

1. **Cooling-off period:** You may cancel within 7 days of purchase for a full refund, provided the service has not commenced.
2. **After cooling-off:** Refunds are discretionary. Contact connect@numaway.com within 30 days.
3. **Non-refundable:** Government fees (visa applications, university application fees) paid on your behalf are non-refundable once submitted.

## Process
Email connect@numaway.com with your order reference. Refunds are processed within 10 business days to the original payment method.

## FCCPA Compliance
This policy complies with the Nigerian Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018.
