# Privacy Policy — Numaway Education Services Limited

**Effective date:** 2026-04-27
**Jurisdiction:** Nigeria (primary) | EU (GDPR applicable for EU-resident users)
**Status:** DRAFT — Counsel review required before production deployment

## Summary
Numaway collects personal data to match students with universities and provide study abroad guidance. We do not sell data. We comply with the Nigerian Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU-resident users.

## Data We Collect
- Contact information (name, email, phone)
- Academic background and study preferences
- Consultation records and communications
- Technical data (IP address, browser, usage analytics)

## How We Use It
- Providing counselling and application support
- Matching to suitable universities and courses
- Sending service updates and educational content (opt-in)
- Improving platform features

## Retention
We retain personal data for 3 years after your last interaction, then delete or anonymise.

## Your Rights (NDPA / GDPR)
- Access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Lodge a complaint with NITDA (Nigeria) or your local DPA (EU)
