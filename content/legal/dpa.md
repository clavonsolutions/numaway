# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — Numaway Education Services Limited

**For:** Institutional Partners and University Partners
**Effective date:** 2026-04-27
**Status:** DRAFT — Counsel and DPO review required before deployment

## Purpose
This DPA governs the processing of personal data shared between Numaway Education Services Limited ("Numaway") and institutional partners for student referral and application purposes.

## Scope
Applies when a partner institution receives student personal data (name, contact, academic records) from Numaway for the purpose of university admissions.

## Obligations of Numaway
- Process data only for agreed purposes
- Maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures
- Notify partner of data breaches within 72 hours
- Provide audit trail on request

## Obligations of Partner
- Process student data only for admissions purposes
- Not retain data beyond the agreed period (typically 12 months post-decision)
- Comply with applicable data protection law in the partner's jurisdiction

## Governing Law
Nigerian law. GDPR Article 28 compliance for EU-based partners.
