# Accessibility Statement — Numaway Education Services Limited

**Standard:** WCAG 2.2 Level AA
**Effective date:** 2026-04-27
**Status:** DRAFT

## Our Commitment
Numaway is committed to making numaway.com accessible to all users, including those with disabilities.

## Current Status
We are actively working to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Known areas of improvement:
- Colour contrast is being audited against brand palette
- ARIA labels are being added to all interactive elements
- Keyboard navigation is being tested across all pages

## Assistive Technology
The platform is designed to work with:
- Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
- Keyboard-only navigation
- Browser zoom up to 200%

## Feedback
If you experience accessibility barriers, contact: connect@numaway.com

We aim to respond within 5 business days.
