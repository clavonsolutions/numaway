# Acceptable Use Policy — Numaway Education Services Limited

**Effective date:** 2026-04-27
**Status:** DRAFT — Counsel review required

## Permitted Uses
- Researching study abroad options
- Communicating with counsellors in good faith
- Submitting genuine applications and documents

## Prohibited Uses
- Submitting false, misleading, or fraudulent documents
- Impersonating another person
- Attempting to circumvent platform security
- Spamming or harassing Numaway staff or other users
- Scraping or bulk-extracting platform data
- Using Sage AI to generate fraudulent statements or documents

## Enforcement
Violations may result in account suspension, referral to relevant authorities, and legal action. Numaway co-operates with universities and immigration authorities to prevent fraud.

## Reporting
Report suspected misuse to connect@numaway.com.
